Privacy Policy

Application: Toolpath Simulation with STL / CNC CAD Viewer
Package Name: com.aistudio.toolpath.xrqz
Effective Date: September 13, 2026
100% Client-Side Local Processing
Summary: Our application respects your privacy. All your CNC CAD blueprints (.dxf, .dwg), 3D solid mesh files (.stl), ArtCAM relief files (.rlf), and G-code programs (.tap, .nc, .txt) are processed and simulated 100% locally on your device. We do NOT collect, transmit, store, or sell your files or personal data.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect personally identifiable information (PII). In particular:

  • No CAD / CAM / G-Code File Uploads: When you open a .tap, .nc, .dxf, .stl, or .rlf file, it is parsed strictly in-memory inside your device's local memory and rendered using local GPU hardware acceleration. No files or geometries are ever uploaded to any cloud or third-party servers.
  • No Account Required: You do not need to register, create an account, or log in with personal details to use this application.
  • No Personal Files Access: The app only accesses files explicitly chosen by you through the Android Storage Access Framework (SAF) system file picker.

2. Permissions Used by the Application

The application requests only minimal, necessary system permissions to function:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required exclusively to load Google AdMob advertisements and check network reachability.
  • VIBRATE: Used for optional haptic feedback when pressing CAD control buttons or starting/stopping simulations.
  • WAKE_LOCK: Allows the screen to remain active during long continuous CNC toolpath playback without going to sleep.
  • No Sensitive Permissions: The app does not request Camera, Microphone, GPS Location, Contacts, or broad Storage read/write permissions.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

This application displays advertisements served by Google AdMob to keep the application free for users. AdMob may collect and process certain non-personally identifiable diagnostic and device information in accordance with Google's policies, such as:

  • Device identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID - AAID)
  • IP address (for coarse geographic ad serving)
  • Crash data and performance diagnostic metrics

How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

4. Children's Privacy

Our application is designed for general audiences, students, machinists, and engineers. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

5. Security of Your Data

Because all file parsing, 3D toolpath calculations, and CAD rendering happen offline directly on your mobile device, your proprietary CAD designs, NC programs, and machine instructions never leave your smartphone or tablet.
